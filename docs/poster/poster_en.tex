\sect{01}{Introduction and problem}
Small importers in Colombia sell iPhones in pesos (COP) and pay for them in dollars (USD) in the U.S., bearing the exchange-rate risk between the quote and the payment without access to financial hedging.

TRM Predict turns daily public data into a short-term, actionable signal: \textbf{BUY TODAY} or \textbf{WAIT} for the next business day.

\note{Contribution to the Sustainable Development Goals}{%
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{SDG 8:} decent work and economic growth (small businesses).
  \item \textbf{SDG 9:} industry, innovation and cloud infrastructure.
\end{itemize}}
\figcap{A quantitative decision-support tool; it is not financial advice.}

\sect{02}{Data acquisition and preparation}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Time coverage:} 2,159 business days (Sep.\ 2017 -- Oct.\ 2026) with 43 engineered features and no missing values.
  \item \textbf{Multivariate sources:} official TRM (datos.gov.co), 8 global indicators (Yahoo Finance), BTC/ETH (Binance) and financial news (La República).
  \item \textbf{Feature engineering:} lagged returns, rolling volatility (5 and 20 days), calendar variables and outlier control (3$\times$IQR).
\end{itemize}

\note{KDD / CRISP-DM flow and pattern mining}{%
Selection $\rightarrow$ cleaning $\rightarrow$ transformation $\rightarrow$ EDA $\rightarrow$ modeling $\rightarrow$ evaluation.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Segmentation and rules:} PCA, K-Means ($k=4$ regimes), DBSCAN (57 anomalous days) and Apriori / FP-Growth (91 rules; max.\ lift 1.24).
\end{itemize}}

\sect{03}{Methodology and models}
Overall framework based on Design Thinking, with prototyping under KDD / CRISP-DM. Strict chronological split: 1,727 training days and 432 test days (Dec.\ 2024 -- Oct.\ 2026) with time-series cross-validation.

{\centering\fontsize{19}{24}\selectfont\bfseries\color{navy}
Public data (APIs + scraping) $\rightarrow$ Features (43) $\rightarrow$ Training and test (time CV) $\rightarrow$ Product (API + dashboard)\par}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Tasks:} daily direction, expected return and large-move alert ($>0.86\%$).
  \item \textbf{Models:} logistic regression, k-NN, SVM, Random Forest, AdaBoost and Gradient Boosting, explained with SHAP/LIME.
  \item \textbf{Direction:} Random Forest selected by CV (0.582). Gradient Boosting reached a slightly higher test AUC (0.566).
\end{itemize}

\centerline{\includegraphics[width=0.9\linewidth]{model_auc.png}}
\figcap{Out-of-sample ROC-AUC by model.}

\note{Return regression}{%
Random Forest RMSE: 0.00759 \,·\, random walk: 0.00769 \,·\, $R^2$: 0.017.\\
A 1.3\% RMSE improvement over assuming tomorrow equals today.}

\note{Interpretability with SHAP and LIME}{%
The most influential variables are the USD/COP--TRM gap (spot\_gap), the lagged TRM return (lag 1) and the change in the dollar index (DXY). It supports the decision without replacing financial judgment.}

\sect{04}{Results and analysis}
\textbf{Test performance (432 days)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Direction model (RF): AUC 0.555 \,|\, accuracy 53.2\% \,|\, F1 0.514.
  \item Majority baseline: 54.6\% \,|\, Brier score: 0.2474.
  \item Risk alert model ($>0.86\%$): AUC 0.585.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{K-Means regimes ($k=4$):} Calm (752 d), Risk appetite (754 d), Risk aversion (606 d) and Crisis (47 d). Silhouette = 0.116.

\textbf{Historical backtest:} with $P(\text{down})\geq 55\%$ the rule recommended WAIT on 101/432 days with 63.4\% accuracy. Mean saving: COP 2,314.7 per USD 1,000 per day (vs.\ COP 1,383.3 at random).

\sect{05}{Cloud architecture and validation}
{\centering\fontsize{19}{24}\selectfont\bfseries\color{navy}
EventBridge (schedule) $\rightarrow$ Lambda (ingestion / ETL) $\rightarrow$ S3 (raw data + replica) $\rightarrow$ RDS PostgreSQL $\rightarrow$ EC2 + ALB (FastAPI / ASG)\par}

Infrastructure as code (Terraform): AWS Lambda, EventBridge, S3 (CRR), RDS PostgreSQL, EC2, ALB, Auto Scaling, ECR, CloudWatch, SSM and Budgets.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Load test:} 4,543 requests in 14 min with 0 errors (auto-scaling up to 4 EC2 instances).
  \item \textbf{High availability:} 100\% success (378/378 probes) with 2 instances; full recovery in 139 s.
  \item \textbf{AWS operating cost:} estimated USD 21 for the whole semester running in sessions (USD 63.6/month always on). Actual spend (Sep.--Nov.\ 2026): USD 202.02 (USD 179.98 covered by credits).
\end{itemize}

\note{Use case: the importer's decision}{%
Computes today's and tomorrow's cost in COP from the Apple price, parcel-forwarding fee, bank surcharge and taxes, recommending WAIT when $P(\text{down})\geq 55\%$ outside a crisis.}

\sect{06}{Conclusions and limitations}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{End-to-end integration:} automated ingestion, explainable ML, REST API, web dashboard and AWS infrastructure as code.
  \item \textbf{Moderate predictive power:} directional accuracy (53.2\%) does not beat the majority baseline (54.6\%), and the regression lowers RMSE by 1.3\% against the random walk.
  \item \textbf{Regime sensitivity:} the rule reached 63.4\% accuracy in a test period dominated by a falling dollar.
  \item \textbf{Future work:} live news sentiment, real card and parcel-forwarding fees, and validation in rising periods.
\end{itemize}

\note{Practical contribution}{An accessible prototype that turns daily public FX data into a COP cost comparison and a transparent buy/wait signal for small importers.}
\note{Responsible use}{Predictions are probabilistic and do not include every bank, card, parcel-forwarding or tax charge. Check real rates before operating.}
\note{Next steps}{Walk-forward evaluation across exchange-rate regimes, probability calibration and field validation with real importers.}
\note{Cloud learning}{Terraform makes deployments reproducible; load and failure tests validate elasticity and resilience, while cost control is key.}
